Features

Everything your journey needs, in one place.

01

Prayer Times

Accurate times calculated on your device. Calculation method auto-detected for your country. Reminders that arrive when they should.

02

Qibla Direction

A live compass pointing toward Makkah, wherever you are. Works the moment you open the app — no account, no setup.

03

Mosque Finder

Find mosques worldwide. See Jummah times, khateeb names, and live announcements from your local mosque.

04

Halal Food

Discover certified halal restaurants, groceries, and butchers near you — with full certification details on every listing.

05

Ingredient Scanner

Scan any product barcode to check for haram ingredients — E-numbers, pork derivatives, alcohol, and more. Works offline.

06

Quran & Duas

All 114 surahs with Arabic, transliteration, and translation. A full duas library and a fresh reminder every day.
